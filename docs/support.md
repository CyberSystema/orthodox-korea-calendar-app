# Support — OK Calendar

Thank you for using the **OK Calendar**. This page explains how to get help, report a problem, or send us a suggestion.

The Service is developed and actively maintained by **CyberSystema** for the **Orthodox Metropolis of Korea and Exarchate of Japan**.

## How to get help

Most questions can be answered with a quick check inside the app:

1. **Open the app or website.**
2. **Make sure your device is online** the first time you open it (afterwards the calendar works offline).
3. **Check that notifications are enabled** in your device settings if you would like to receive reminders.
4. **Update the app** from the App Store or Google Play if a new version is available.

If something still does not work, we will be glad to help.

## Contact

For all support requests, including:

- a feast, fast, or parish event that looks wrong,
- the calendar not loading,
- notifications not arriving,
- crashes or unexpected behaviour,
- accessibility issues,
- privacy questions,
- general feedback or suggestions,

please contact us:

- **Email (technical operator, CyberSystema):** _support@cybersystema.com_
- **Website:** [https://orthodox-korea-calendar.pages.dev](https://orthodox-korea-calendar.pages.dev)
- **Through the Metropolis:** the Orthodox Metropolis of Korea and Exarchate of Japan can also forward your message to us.

We aim to acknowledge support requests within **two business days**.

## When you write to us

To help us help you faster, please include — where possible — the following information:

- which platform you are using (iOS, Android, or web);
- your device model and operating-system version (for example, _iPhone 14, iOS 18.2_);
- the version number of the app (shown at the bottom of the app's **Settings** screen, or on the App Store / Google Play listing);
- a short description of what you expected to happen, what actually happened, and the steps you took before the problem appeared;
- a screenshot or a screen recording, if appropriate.

Please **do not** send passwords, payment information, or any other sensitive personal data — we never ask for them.

## Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability in the app, the website, or the backend, please report it privately and responsibly to **security@cybersystema.com**. Do not disclose the issue publicly until we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate it. We will respond promptly and credit responsible disclosure when a fix is published.

## Service status and known issues

If you are having trouble loading the calendar or receiving notifications, the cause is occasionally a temporary outage of one of the underlying services — **GitHub** (which hosts the public calendar data), **Cloudflare** (which serves parish events and notification registration), or the **Apple Push Notification service** / **Firebase Cloud Messaging** (which deliver notifications). We monitor the Service continuously and will publish notices in the app when sustained disruptions occur.

## Cost

The OK Calendar is offered **free of charge**, with no advertising and no in-app purchases. If you would like to support the work of the Metropolis, please contact your parish.
